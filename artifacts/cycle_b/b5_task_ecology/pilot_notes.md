# B5 validation pilots

- The seven-task seed-11 pilot excluded argmax-position (validation accuracy 0.567) and
  argmin-position (0.583). Its frozen-test measurements were not retained or used in claims.
- The five-task mixed-identifiability pilot used five seeds, but validation task means ranged from
  0.813 to 0.917 and no pair met the registered seed-wise competence rule.
- Confirmatory revision 3 therefore uses maximum, minimum, first, middle, and last with only the
  direct high-identifiability instruction wording. Pilot checkpoints are retained for audit only.
